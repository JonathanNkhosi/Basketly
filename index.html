About

Built from a simple idea: make everyday grocery shopping easier for people in Lusaka.

R
Ryachi
Creator of Basketly

Basketly is a concept and prototype — a first look at what a simpler, more connected grocery experience for Lusaka could feel like. It is not yet an established e-commerce platform, and the stores shown here are presented for demonstration as retailers Basketly hopes to work with.
